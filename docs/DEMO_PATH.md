# The webinar's demo path, 10 November 2026

> **A draft for the owner's approval.** Nothing here is final until the owner
> says so.

Fifteen stops, in the order of the deck's screenshots, with a search added
between the first two so the bill is reached the way a reader would reach it.
Each is an address on graniterecord.org and one sentence to say there. To
rehearse before the release reaches the live site, put
`https://dry-run.graniterecord.pages.dev` in front of the address instead.

Every address was opened on that preview at a phone's width (390 px) on 25
October 2026: each loaded, with no sideways scroll and no script error.
Stops 10, 11 and 15 came with the deck's three new slides and were opened the
same way on graniterecord.org itself on 5 November 2026, because the webinar
falls before the next release. Open each again on graniterecord.org, at phone
width, the morning of the webinar.

1. `/`
   "Granite Record is New Hampshire government in one place: every bill
   since 1989, every roll call since 1999, and each hearing's recording
   where there is one."
2. `/search?q=HB%20282` (type HB 282 in the home page's search box)
   "One box takes a bill number, a topic, a name or a town, and puts this
   term's bill first."
3. `/bill/2025/hb282`
   "A bill's page: its sponsor, the committees that heard it, the official
   analysis, and each step from introduced to law."
4. `/bill/2025/hb282/hearings`
   "Every hearing on it, with how many signed in for and against, and the
   recording starts where the chair took the bill up."
5. `/bill/2025/hb282/votes`
   "Every vote on it, the motion in plain words, and on a roll call how each
   party voted."
6. `/committee/H11`
   "The committee that heard it: its chair, its members, where it meets, and
   its bills and meetings."
7. `/laws/281-a#s32-a`
   "The law the bill changed, whose source note names the 2025 chapter the
   bill became."
8. `/legislator/alice-wade-straf-15`
   "A legislator's term in numbers: days attended, roll calls voted on, bills
   filed and passed, and every vote."
9. `/town/dover-ward-2#map`
   "Your town: who represents it, on a map of its districts, the seats it
   shares with its neighbors included."
10. `/officials`
    "Every town and ward has a page like that one, with five tabs; and the
    Officials page puts every district in the state on one map: choose a kind
    of seat, then a district, to see who represents it."
11. `/resources`
    "Resources gathers the how-to guides, short Learn pages with a glossary,
    and where the state publishes things itself."
12. `/guides/testifying`
    "The guides say how to take part: which meetings you may attend, and the
    one where you may speak."
13. `/calendar/2026-W08`
    "The calendar shows a week of hearings and sessions, and narrows to a
    chamber, a committee or a bill."
14. `/bills?term=2027-requests&q=LSR%202027-0002` (press Follow on the card)
    "And you can follow what matters to you by email, daily or weekly: this
    request for next year, and the bill it becomes."
15. `/bill/2026/hb100` (open its Follow panel)
    "A bill still moving, a law, a legislator, a committee or a bill request:
    each has a Follow button that says what it will send. A bill that has
    become law, like HB 282, has none."
