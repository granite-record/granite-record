# Interim study statements on the Reports tab

Screenshots of the card feat/interim-study-statements adds (818160f), from a
full build of the 29 October 2026 kit with GRANITE_BUILD_DATE=2026-10-28, the
Reports tab opened, at 390 and 1280 pixels wide, light and dark.

- `2024_hb1530_*` -- HB 1530 of 2024: a statement with its member, reasons and
  vote (House Calendar 40, 2024), after the committee's February report.
- `2004_hb541_*` -- HB 541 of 2004: the calendar prints no recommendation, so
  the card shows the House docket's and says so.
- `2010_hb1547_*` -- HB 1547 of 2010: a recommendation alone, no reasons and
  no vote. The tab's existing line for that case says "the recommendation and
  the vote"; the vote part is app.js's to correct.
