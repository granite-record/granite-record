## Neutrality and Methods

I'm a sitting member of the House, so the site keeps to these rules.
**[owner to confirm]**

- Nothing on it is editorialized. Dates, sponsors, vote tallies, committee
  assignments and hearing times are taken directly from the official record.
  Committee reports appear as filed, in the committee's own words, and
  summaries of a bill's progress are generated from the record by software.
- A report made with *Report a problem* holds nothing that identifies who
  sent it, so every one is checked against the official record in the same
  way, and corrected where the record bears it out. An email to
  contact@graniterecord.org is handled the same way, whoever sends it.
  **[owner to confirm: the second sentence]**
- On the Learn pages, where a concept needs an example bill, town or
  legislator, the site uses a less polarizing one when there is a choice. A
  list taken from the record, such as the hearings with the most sign-ins, is
  shown as the record has it. **[owner to confirm]**

Each step-by-step guide links the official page that has the final word, and
says when it was last checked against it.

Hearing timestamps are measured against proceedings a person timed by
watching the recording. On the ones the method places, the median is out by
[[median]]. [How the record is built](data.html#how-built), on the Data page,
gives every figure.

Corrections will be listed in a public corrections log.
**[owner to confirm; link when the log is published]**
