# The guides bundle for the 1 November release: the hub

`bundle/guides` (commit 189618e, on dev 5dec791) carries the five guide
commits of 29 October. They are:

- the laws that changed a chapter, by year, with one year menu, and each
  citation kept whole on a phone;
- steps in parts, each numbered from 1;
- the parts review's fixes;
- the How-To Guides tab laid out as the Learn tab is.

`hub-guides-<390|1280>-<light|dark>.png` are the Resources page's How-To Guides
tab, full page, as the bundle builds it. They were built on preflight's
fixture with the kit's statute table (`db/NH_RSA.psv`), so every guide the
real build publishes is published here too: 49 guides, 6 under Most Common,
11 topic groups, no sideways scroll at 390. The logo is blank because it is
not in the repository.
