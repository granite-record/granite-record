How New Hampshire works / Who writes the law

The General Court

New Hampshire has the largest state legislature in the country and one of the least paid. Both facts shape everything else on this site.

400 representatives, one for roughly every 3,444 residents Counted from the 2025–2026 member roster, September 2026

A representative is paid $100 a year plus mileage, a figure set by the Constitution and unchanged since 1889. Nobody serves in the House as a career, which means the body is composed largely of the retired, the self-employed and the independently supported, and it turns over heavily every two years.

The Senate is 24 members, elected from districts of roughly 56,000 people each, on the same two-year cycle. A senator is paid the same $100.
